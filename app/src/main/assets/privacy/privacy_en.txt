Easy Ledger (轻记账) Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-13
Publisher: Ch Etang (Etai Apps)

In one sentence
This app works offline with no account and no server we operate. By default we do not collect or upload personal information. Data stays on your device; files leave the phone only when you back up, export, or share them yourself.

On-device app data
· Ledger entries, categories, accounts, budgets, and reminder settings
· Notes and tags
· Receipt photos you attach to entries (if you capture or pick from the gallery)
· Language, theme, and other UI preferences (DataStore / local prefs)

Content you export or share
When you use backup, CSV/JSON export, or share a monthly report, the app creates files that appear only where you save or send them. We do not upload them for you.

What we do not collect
· No sign-up or sign-in
· No ad, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs (as reflected in this release’s manifest and dependencies)
· No INTERNET permission; normal use does not require a network, and we have no backend that receives your ledger data

Permissions (see the system install prompt and merged manifest for this build)
· Camera (only when you tap to take a receipt photo): used for photos you choose to capture; stored in the app’s private storage
· Photos / media access (only when you pick from the gallery): for images you explicitly select; we do not scan your gallery in the background
· Optional hardware: camera (android.hardware.camera, not required)
· AndroidX libraries may merge signature-level permissions such as DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION for in-app component communication only

Children
We do not direct the app at children or collect children’s data for our services. A parent or guardian can uninstall the app or manage data on the device.

Security and device changes
· On-device security depends mainly on your lock screen and system settings
· Uninstalling usually deletes app data; back up before reinstalling or switching phones
· Release-signed builds may not overwrite older debug builds (com.qingjizhang.app.debug); export JSON before migrating

Third parties
Download stores (GitHub Releases, Coolapk, Google Play, etc.) have their own policies, separate from in-app data handling.

Open-source libraries are used under their licenses; we do not use them to add tracking.

Changes
If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date and note it on the website or in release notes where practical.
